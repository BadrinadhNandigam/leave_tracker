Privacy Policy – Leave Notifier

Last updated: November 2026

  • No account, no server. The app runs entirely in your browser or phone.
  • Data stored: team names, phone numbers, leave records and alarm settings stay only in your device's local storage. We never receive, collect or sell this data.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • WhatsApp: tapping Send opens WhatsApp (wa.me) with a pre-filled message. You send it yourself; WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Calendar (optional): the "Phone alarm" button opens a Google Calendar link with the leave title and dates.
  • Deleting data: More → Clear log / Clear numbers, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Contact: [your email]

Leave Notifier is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
