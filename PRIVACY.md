# Privacy Policy – Leave Notifier

Last updated: November 2026

- **No account, no server.** Leave Notifier runs entirely in your browser/phone.
- **Data stored:** team names, phone numbers, leave records and alarm settings are saved only in your device's local storage. We never receive, collect or sell this data.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.**
- **WhatsApp:** tapping Send opens WhatsApp (wa.me) with a pre-filled message. Messages are sent by you through WhatsApp and are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
- **Google Calendar (optional):** the "Phone alarm" button opens a Google Calendar link containing the leave title and dates.
- **Deleting data:** use More → Clear log / Clear numbers, or clear your browser's site data.
- **Contact:** [your email]
